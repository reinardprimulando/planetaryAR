Anchor accuracy is limited by your phone's GPS, roughly ±5 m. Jupiter and beyond land reliably; the inner four are approximate. Set the anchor while standing still with a clear view of the sky, and give it thirty seconds to settle.

Heading trim corrects a mis-calibrated compass. Stand at the Sun, point the phone at a landmark whose true bearing you know, and drag until it lines up. Or walk to where you believe a planet is and tap Align while aiming at it.

Solar Walk

One volleyball Sun,
seven hundred metres of sky.

Plant the Sun at your feet. Every planet is placed at its real position for right now, at one scale, on real ground. Walk to Jupiter. It takes a while — that is the point.

Needs camera, location and motion permission. Works outdoors, in daylight, with room to walk.
